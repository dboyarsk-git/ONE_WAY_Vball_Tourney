Everything You Need to Know

ONE WAY Conference

📍 Location & Schedule

  • Location: Church of New Hope
  • Check-in: 8:00 AM
  • Captains Meeting: 8:45 AM
  • Pool Play: 9:00 AM
  • Teams more than 10 minutes late for a scheduled match forfeit that match.

Registration & Tournament Format

  • Registration is capped at 16 teams.
  • $15 per player / $60 per team.
  • 4 players per team, 4 pools of 4, 4 courts.
  • Each team receives 3 pool-play matches.
  • The top 2 teams from each pool advance to the 8-team bracket.
  • All 4 players must personally confirm their roster spot and agree to the rules before the team becomes fully registered.

⚠️ Non-Negotiables

  • Modest shorts.
  • Shirts must fully cover the stomach, including when arms are raised.
  • Shirts must remain on at all times.
  • No sleeveless shirts.
  • No alcohol, smoking, vaping, or drugs.
  • No cursing or inappropriate language.
  • Violations WILL result in disqualification.

Conduct & Sportsmanship

  • This is an official Christian church event hosted for the ONE WAY Conference at CNH.
  • Players are expected to represent that through appropriate clothing, language, conduct, and sportsmanship.
  • Disrespectful comments toward women or other players will not be tolerated.
  • No excessive arguing, trash talking, or inappropriate behavior.

Scoring Format

  • Pool Play: 1 set to 25.
  • 25-point hard cap: first team to 25 wins.
  • Bracket Play: best 2 out of 3 sets.
  • Sets 1 & 2 are played to 21.
  • Set 3 is played to 15.
  • Completed sets and matches lock automatically.

Game Rules

  • 4 players on the court.
  • Maximum of 3 contacts per side.
  • A block does not count as one of the 3 contacts.
  • No catches, carries, lifts, or throws.
  • No open-hand tips/dinks; roll shots and knuckles are allowed.
  • The ball may be played off the net.
  • No interfering net contact or unsafe crossing under the net.
  • Overhand and underhand serves are allowed.
  • No blocking or attacking a serve above the net.
  • If a call cannot be clearly determined, the point is replayed.
